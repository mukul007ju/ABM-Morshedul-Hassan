About me

I, A B M Morshedul Hassan, completed my undergraduate studies in Urban and Regional Planning at Jahangirnagar University and am currently pursuing a Master’s degree in Applied Geoinformatics at Paris Lodron University of Salzburg. I am a member of the Bangladesh Institute of Planners and a registered Town Planner with the Rajdhani Unnayan Kartripakkha (RAJUK). I am also a Master Planner and Utility Mapping Professional. Previously, I worked on the preparation of private residential land development plans for several township projects in Bangladesh, as well as on the design and implementation of water supply networks for the Dhaka Water Supply and Sewerage Authority. I have also been involved in master plan preparation, including planning compliance and permission processes for large-scale civil and military establishments in Bangladesh and Kuwait. In addition, I have practical experience in GIS database management, map preparation, and web-based mapping applications.
